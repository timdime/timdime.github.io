About

Technology leadership for the trading floor.

I lead the infrastructure, support, and trading desk technology organization at one of Chicago's premier proprietary trading firms. My focus is the intersection of resilient systems, strong teams, and the operational excellence that high-stakes markets demand.

As Director of IT Operations & Infrastructure at Belvedere Trading, I oversee the Infrastructure, Release, Support, and Trading Desk teams. I own the firm's production environment, manage cross-functional projects across the business, and align technology investment with strategic roadmaps.

I joined Belvedere as a Systems & Network Administrator and have grown alongside the firm — building the Business Intelligence team from the ground up, leading more than ten datacenter and office migrations, and embedding external trading groups into our environment. I became a Capital Partner in 2011.

Across every role I prioritize the same things: low-latency, well-monitored systems; clear, automated workflows; and teams that own their outcomes.

What I bring to the table

  • Deep operational experience in low-latency, regulated environments
  • A bias for automation, observability, and clear runbooks
  • Multi-site infrastructure across Chicago, Boulder, NY, and Singapore
  • Vendor & contract negotiation across hardware, market data, and connectivity
  • Coaching and growing technical leaders within the organization
